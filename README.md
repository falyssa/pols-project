# 427÷1

A choice game for POLS 1101 (Dr. Newton) at the University of Georgia.

**Topic:** Businesses in Georgia must limit the pay of their highest paid employees to no more than 100 times the pay of the lowest full-time employee of that company.

**Position:** For.

You live one year as a full-time Home Depot worker in Athens, Georgia, twice: once in Georgia as it is today, and once three years after the pay cap became law. Same person, same bills, same choices. Every number in the game links to its source.

**Play it:** https://falyssa.github.io/pols-project/

This is a class project. It is not affiliated with or endorsed by The Home Depot, and the characters are fictional.
